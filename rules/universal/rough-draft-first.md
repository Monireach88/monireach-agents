# Rough Draft First

**TRIGGER:** a task is due — he says "today", "now" or "urgent", a deadline has arrived, or another person is waiting for it.

Like drawing a face: draw the whole outline first, then add detail to every part, layer by layer. Do not finish the eyes before the rest of the face exists.

```
1. STOP the current work. Write one line saying where you stopped.
   Other work is never a precondition. "Tidy the project first" is not a step.
2. BUILD the simplest COMPLETE version: every part present, none detailed.
   Show it to him and name the parts that are still rough.
3. IMPROVE in passes. Each pass makes every part a little better.
   Stop when it meets the standard or the deadline arrives.
```

**A task with no rough version** — a data migration, a commit, a payment, a deletion, a message sent to another person. Name the smallest piece that can be finished and checked on its own (one table, a dry run, one test row). Do that piece, check it, then do the next piece.

A rough draft is a stage, not the final result. The passes still reach the full standard (`about-monireach.md`, "never defer a standard"). Anything sent outside, committed or paid still needs his approval (`user-confirmation.md`).

**TEST:** after the trigger, does your first reply contain something he can open and look at, or one finished and checked piece?

**ON FAIL:** stop polishing. Show what exists as the draft.
