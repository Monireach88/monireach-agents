# Rough Draft First

Trigger: the task is due now. He says "today", "now" or "urgent", a deadline has arrived, or another person is waiting.

1. Stop the current work and write one line saying where you stopped. Do not tidy anything first.
2. Build the simplest complete version: every part present, none polished. Show it to him and name the parts that are still rough.
3. Improve every part in passes until it meets the standard or the deadline arrives.

A task with no rough version (a data migration, a commit, a payment, a deletion, a message to another person): do the smallest piece that can be finished and checked on its own, check it, then do the next piece.

A draft is a stage, not the result: the passes still reach the full standard, and anything sent, committed or paid still needs his approval.

Test: your first reply after the trigger contains something he can open, or one finished and checked piece.
