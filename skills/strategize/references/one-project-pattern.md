# The One Project Pattern

## When to Use

The user has 3+ interconnected personal/professional goals and limited time, and is
treating them as sequential ("fix X first, then Y, then Z"). The sequence guarantees
failure because life doesn't pause between phases.

Applies when goals include some combination of:
- Income / career advancement
- Skill building (especially communication/articulation)
- Credential completion (degree, certification)
- Portfolio/audience building

And constraints include:
- Full-time job consuming weekdays
- Limited discretionary hours (≤15/week)
- Confidence or communication anxiety

## Core Mechanism

Find ONE activity that forces all goals forward simultaneously. The activity must have:

1. **A hard weekly deadline** — publish-or-be-embarrassed. The deadline IS the point;
   it defeats perfectionism by removing the option to polish indefinitely.
2. **A public output** — blog post, video, newsletter, open-source contribution.
   Private practice doesn't create accountability.
3. **Direct connection to the domain expertise goal** — the content IS the research,
   not separate from it.
4. **Compounding value** — each output builds audience/credibility that makes future
   outputs more valuable, not less.

## The Weekly Rhythm

1. **Pick one concept** from the domain (research area, technical skill, etc.)
2. **Study until you can explain to a beginner** — Feynman Technique. Draw the diagram.
3. **Produce the output** — 800-1200 word post or 5-minute video. Concrete example first,
   then principle, then why it matters.
4. **Publish.** Same day/time every week. No exceptions.

## Compound Timeline

| Phase | Communication | Knowledge | Income Potential |
|-------|--------------|-----------|-----------------|
| Month 1 (4 outputs) | Painful but doable | Domain lit review underway | Zero |
| Month 3 (12 outputs) | Finding voice, less anxiety | Ready to draft thesis/proposal | Growing audience, some inbound |
| Month 6 (24 outputs) | Confident, natural | Proposal submitted | Credible enough for consulting/contracts |

## Why It Works

- **Eliminates retreat** (Principle 12): public deadline removes the perfectionism escape hatch
- **Let them sell themselves** (Principle 23): clients find you through your content already convinced
- **Know yourself** (Principle 25): leverages precision/rigor as clarity, not paralysis

## Anti-Patterns

- Starting with the hardest concept — pick something you already know 70% of, not 10%
- Waiting until you're "ready" — the first 4 outputs will be bad. That's the point.
- Doing it privately first — private practice doesn't create the stakes that force growth
- Optimizing for audience in month 1 — the first 10 outputs are gym reps, not lead generation
